## 1. Shape: a characteristic list, as in a factor panel

The format follows an equity characteristics panel, where one row is a security-month
and the columns are coded characteristics such as `ret_12_1` or `ivol_capm_21d`. Here:

- **Unit of observation:** one `(cell, body)` row. Cells are Earth H3 cells or Moon/Mars
  site cells, with the same feature codes on both sides wherever the physics allows.
- **Code = measure + scale.** For example, `slope_100m` is the slope over a 100 m
  baseline, and `slope_2_5km` is the slope over a 2–5 km baseline. A different
  baseline gives a different feature. This matters because MSL alone sets four
  different slope limits at four length scales.
- **Each row is a feature card.** It gives the definition, unit, scale, target-body
  source (a registry `datasetId` from #4 where one exists), the published
  engineering threshold with a source key, the Earth counterpart feature and
  dataset, and `earth_match`.
- **No composite.** Features feed per-dimension losses only (AGENTS.md rule 4).
  `mismatch` rows are never scored. They populate the "Earth cannot rehearse this"
  list (S1 / S6f).

`earth_match` values:

| Value | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| `direct` | Same quantity, same parameter type on Earth (e.g. DEM slope at a matched baseline, observed LST extremes, broadband albedo) | 15 |
| `proxy` | Related physics, but a different parameter type or unit (e.g. apparent thermal inertia vs. thermal inertia; EMIT library band depth vs. CRISM fixed-wavelength index; 5-band ASTER emissivity vs. TES/Diviner spectra) | 21 |
| `weak` | Only loosely analogous (e.g. Earth horizon masking vs. the Earth-visibility comms constraint; permafrost extent vs. ice-stability depth) | 5 |
| `gap` | No Earth dataset in the registry (e.g. rock abundance, Lyman-α frost, gamma-ray Th/K, caves) | 8 |
| `mismatch` | Permanent breakdown: gravity, pressure/EDL atmosphere, radiation, ~100 K cold traps, vertical EDL winds | 6 |

The `gap` rows matter for scoring. A dimension that depends on them cannot pass ARL
gates 1–3 from global data. Those features need site evidence (gate 4, literature or
fieldwork), which is the honest version of "not well characterized" (brief F4).

### Spectral features: same physics, different parameter types (proxy)

v0 called these the strongest `direct` pairs. The #7 review showed that the
quantities differ, so v1 marks them `proxy`:

- **CRISM ↔ EMIT (VSWIR band depths).** CRISM summary parameters are fixed-wavelength
  band-depth and shape indices. EMIT L2B (`emit-l2b-min-v002`, CMR
  `C4079846859-LPCLOUD`, from 2022-08-09) reports a band depth for its best-fit
  library minerals, such as kaolinite, montmorillonite, chlorite, gypsum, calcite
  and dolomite. Both detect the same absorptions, but the parameter types differ, so
  values are not comparable one to one. Comparing them needs either a CRISM-style
  index computed on EMIT L2A reflectance, or a mineral-presence comparison.
- **TES and Diviner ↔ ASTER GED (TIR emissivity).** ASTER GED (`aster-ged-ag100`)
  has 5 broad TIR bands. TES deconvolves about 140 channels into mineral fractions,
  and Diviner fits a Christiansen-feature position. Five bands support only a
  band-shape proxy.

**CRISM parameter check.** Names and rationales were read from Table 3-12 of the
CRISM Software Interface Specification (key CRISM-SIS, §4). The formula reference
PDS-CRISM-SPREF says the formulations follow Viviano et al. (2014) "with minor
adjustments". The paper itself was not read: it is paywalled. All names in the CSV
are present in both PDS documents.

| Feature | Parameter (SIS #) | SIS rationale | SIS caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mafic_bd_1um` | OLINDEX3 (13), LCPINDEX2 (14), HCPINDEX2 (15) | olivines; pyroxene (LCP/HCP) | HCP, Fe-phyllosilicates (OLINDEX3) |
| — (removed) | BD1300 (16) | plagioclase with Fe²⁺ substitution | Fe-rich olivine can be > 0 |
| `crism_hydration` | BD1900_2 (24) | H₂O in minerals | weakly sensitive to low-Ca pyroxene, glass, H₂O ice |
| `crism_al_oh` | BD2210_2 (32) | Al-OH minerals | gypsum, alunite |
| `crism_fe_mg_smectite` | D2300 (38) | Mg,Fe-OH minerals | Mg-carbonate; also CO₂ ice |
| `crism_sulfate` | SINDEX2 (40) | mono- and polyhydrated sulfates | — |
| `crism_carbonate` | MIN2295_2480 (42), MIN2345_2537 (43), BD2500_2 (44) | Mg-carbonate; Ca/Fe-carbonate; Mg-carbonate | hydroxylated silicates (42, 43; prehnite, serpentine for 43); some zeolites (44) |

v0 errors fixed:
- `mafic_bd_1um` cited BD1300, which is a plagioclase parameter.
- `crism_sulfate` said "polyhydrated" only; SINDEX2 covers mono- and polyhydrated
  sulfates.
- `crism_carbonate` omitted the Ca/Fe parameter MIN2345_2537.
- **Caveat on mafic minerals.** EMIT's mineral library was designed for dust-source
  minerals, so olivine/pyroxene (`mafic_bd_1um`) is only a `proxy` until EMIT's
  coverage of those minerals is confirmed.

## 3. Published engineering thresholds (quoted from primary sources)

| Constraint | Mars (MSL guide v2) | Moon (Artemis III) |
|---|---|---|
| Latitude | 60°S–60°N | within 6° of the south pole |
| Elevation | ≤ +2 km vs. MOLA areoid (baseline); −2.0 km contingency. InSight: ≤ −2.5 km | — |
| Slope, descent scale | ≤ 3° over 2–5 km; ≤ 5° over 200–500 m; ≤ 15° over 20–40 m | — |
| Slope, touchdown scale | ≤ 15° over 5 m | < 10° over 100 m (8° continuous-slope contours shown) |
| Slope, surface ops | rover static stability ≥ 45°, nominal ops < 30° tilt; design terrain 15% rocks / 5° mean slope / 10% slip | EVA < 20° over 2 km |
| Rocks | P(rock > 0.6 m in 4 m²) < 0.25% ⇒ ~10% cumulative fractional area | not specified in the probed sources |
| Winds | steady horizontal < 30 m/s, vertical < 10 m/s (last km of descent) | — |
| Surface material | Ka-band backscatter > −20 dB; TI < 100 J m⁻² K⁻¹ s⁻½ **and** albedo > 0.25 ⇒ unsuitable (dust) | — |
| Comms / light | — | direct-with-Earth link (no relay); Earth visibility < 25% and solar visibility < 5% reduce usable area; surface lit for the whole stay; land within 100 m of target |

These are mission engineering limits, not analog tolerances. An Earth test site for
`OP-5LM` (Mars EVA/mobility) needs the slope and rock distribution of the target
zone, not the lander's limits. W5 decides how to turn them into envelopes.

## 6. Feature → dataset inventory (v1, #33)

`research/features/build_feature_inventory.py` maps every feature to registry ids
(`data/catalog/`, #32). A `MAP` entry is required for every feature, and an empty
list states explicitly that no registry dataset exists. Every id is asserted to exist
in the registry and to sit on the correct side (an Earth dataset on the Earth side,
for example). The builder writes two files:

- `feature_dataset_inventory.csv`: one row per (feature, dataset, side). Each row
  has a `role` (`primary` / `secondary` / `derived-input` / `context`) and copies the
  registry's `independenceGroup`, licence id, `license.verified`, status and coverage.
  Those copied columns are the registry's claims, not re-probed here.
- `feature_inventory_summary.csv`: one row per feature, with measured datasets per
  side and the number of distinct Earth independence groups among measured
  (`primary` / `secondary`) datasets. Derived inputs and context layers do not count.

The builder output on the #32 registry (68 rows) is reproduced under "Builder output"
in the PR. Of the 49 non-mismatch features, the Earth side has:

| Earth evidence | Features |
|---|---:|
| ≥ 2 independence groups | 17 |
| exactly 1 group | 17 |
| computed only (DEM horizon, LST + albedo) | 6 |
| no Earth dataset in the registry | 9 |

- **No Earth dataset (9):** `lat_abs`, `rock_cfa`, `rock_abund_div`,
  `boulder_density_nac`, `lya_offon`, `tio2_wt`, `oh_3um`, `grs_th_k`,
  `pit_cave_presence`. Any objective that requires these features caps at ARL gate 3
  from global data and needs site evidence (gate 4).
- **Computed only (6):** `ti` (ATI from LST and albedo), `illum_frac_avg`,
  `sun_elev_min`, `earth_vis_frac`, `psr_frac_2km`, `psr_dist_km`. These are
  computed from a DEM horizon plus solar geometry. W5 owns the computation, so they
  are not measurements to look up.
- **Exactly one group (17).** Gate 3 needs at least 2 independent datasets per
  dimension, so the second group has to come from another feature in the same
  dimension, or from site evidence.
- **Independence caveats from #7.** These show up in the groups:
  - The MOD11 products form one group (`modis-tir`). MERRA-2 TS
    (`reanalysis`) is the independent check on the thermal features.
  - NASADEM shares ASTER GDEM lineage, so `srtm-radar` and `optical-stereo` are not
    fully independent for DEM-derived features. The registry still labels them as
    separate groups; W5 must treat NASADEM + ASTER GDEM as one.
  - MCS feeds the LMD dust climatology, so they are not independent on the Mars side.
- **Unused registry rows (10).** These are display and site-context layers: GIBS and
  Trek WMTS, WDPA, travel time, MCD12C1, MOD13C2, MOD11A2, NASA POWER, AG1km. They
  serve gates 5–6 and the atlas, not features.
- **Unverified licences in use.** 13 datasets in the inventory still have
  `license.verified: false`; the summary CSV lists them per feature. W6 must not
  publish a layer derived from one of them until its licence is resolved.
