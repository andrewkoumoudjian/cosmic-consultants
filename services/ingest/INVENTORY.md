## What we used each one for, and what we left out

### `modis-mod11c3`: MODIS/Terra LST/Emissivity Monthly L3 Global 0.05Deg CMG V061

- **How:** Per pixel: max of monthly day LST, min of monthly night LST, mean of (day - night). Then the median over the cell's pixels. Fill 0 and values below 7500 counts are null.
- **Left out / limits:** No daily (MOD11C1) or 8-day files: a yearly range needs only monthly means. One year only.

### `modis-mcd12c1`: MODIS/Terra+Aqua Land Cover Type Yearly L3 Global 0.05Deg CMG V061

- **How:** Mean over the cell's pixels of the IGBP percent layers: 16 barren, 15 snow and ice, 0 water.
- **Left out / limits:** Only Land_Cover_Type_1_Percent layers 0, 15, 16 are read; the file can't be subset on download (HDF4).

### `esa-cci-permafrost-extent-nh`: ESA Permafrost_cci: Permafrost extent for the Northern Hemisphere, v5.0

- **How:** 5x5 block mean to 0.05 deg, then the cell mean. Fill 255 (not modelled) stays null. Model is MODIS-LST-driven, so it's not independent of thermal.
- **Left out / limits:** Other years, and the PFR_uncertainty layer (not used yet).

### `esa-cci-permafrost-extent-antarctica`: ESA Permafrost_cci: Permafrost extent for the Antarctic

- **How:** Same as the NH file.
- **Left out / limits:** Other years.

### `copernicus-dem-glo30`: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public (2021 release), AWS Open Data

- **How:** Slope from the 240 m grid with latitude-aware spacing; per cell: elevation mean/min/max, mean slope, share of pixels under 5 and 15 deg and over 30 deg.
- **Left out / limits:** No full-resolution 30 m reads and no tiles stored (~9 GB streamed, 0 kept). The per-tile ledger stores each array's sha256.

### `merra2-m2tmnxaer`: MERRA-2 tavgM_2d_aer_Nx monthly aerosol diagnostics (dust AOD)

- **How:** Planned: annual mean and max of DUEXTTAU, nearest MERRA-2 grid point.
- **Left out / limits:** BLOCKED: GES DISC answers HTTP 403 for both the files and OPeNDAP until the Earthdata account approves the GES DISC app.
